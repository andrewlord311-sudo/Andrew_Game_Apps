How do you find the note F on the stave (staff)? Melody and Barnaby show the four spaces of the treble stave, which spell the word FACE (F-A-C-E), then the two dots of the bass clef, which sit either side of the F line. A simple music lesson for kids, with a quick quiz at the end.

For children aged about 4 to 7 learning to read music, and the grown-ups helping them.
