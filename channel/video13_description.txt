How long does a note last? Melody and Barnaby meet the crotchet (quarter note), the minim (half note) and the semibreve (whole note), and count how many beats each one lasts in a bar of four. Then clap and count along, and try a quick quiz. A simple music lesson for kids, with the American names too.

For children aged about 4 to 7 learning to read music, and the grown-ups helping them.
