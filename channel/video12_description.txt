What are steps and skips in music? A step moves to the very next note; a skip misses one out. Melody and Barnaby show steps and skips on the piano keyboard and on the stave (staff), then try a quick quiz. A simple music lesson for kids.

For children aged about 4 to 7 learning to read music, and the grown-ups helping them. Ends with a "step or skip?" quiz.
