What is the difference between a melody and harmony? Notes played one after another make a tune, also called a melody. Notes played at the same time make harmony, and a group of notes played together is called a chord. Melody and Barnaby, and their new friend Harmony the rabbit, play both on the piano keyboard and show them on the stave (staff). A simple music lesson for kids, with a quick quiz at the end.

For children aged about 4 to 7 learning to read music, and the grown-ups helping them.
