Where do your fingers go on the piano? Melody and Barnaby show the right hand, with the thumb on Middle C. Each finger has a number (thumb is 1, little finger is 5) and plays one note: C, D, E, F, G. The next finger is a step, and missing a finger out is a skip. See the five notes on the stave, play up and down, then try a quick quiz. A simple music lesson for kids. This video is for the right hand; the left hand comes later.

For children aged about 4 to 7 learning to read music, and the grown-ups helping them.
