## Description (paste into Customisation → Basic info) — live since 22 Oct 2026
Learn music and piano, one little nugget at a time! 🎵

Join Melody the songbird and Barnaby the bear for short, friendly music lessons for kids aged about 4 to 7. Each Nugget teaches one idea: reading the stave (staff), the treble and bass clefs, the music alphabet, high and low, feeling the beat, finding your way round the piano keyboard, C and F, octaves… Then there's a free game to practise it straight away.

🎮 Play the free games (no sign-up, no adverts): https://andrewlord311-sudo.github.io/Andrew_Game_Apps/music/arcade.html

New Nuggets every Wednesday and Saturday. Made for curious young musicians and the parents, teachers and home learners helping them.
