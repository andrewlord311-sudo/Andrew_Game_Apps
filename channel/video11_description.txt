Where is Middle C? Find Middle C on the piano keyboard and on the stave (staff), and see why treble clef is for the right hand and bass clef for the left. Melody and Barnaby show how the keyboard and the music line up. A simple piano and music lesson for kids.

For children aged about 4 to 7 learning to read music and find their way around the piano, and the grown-ups helping them. Ends with a "where is Middle C?" quiz.
