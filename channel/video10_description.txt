What is a bar? Music is split into bars (also called measures) by bar lines, and each bar here holds four beats. Melody and Barnaby count the beat and draw the bar lines. A simple music lesson for kids.

For children aged about 4 to 7 learning to read music, and the grown-ups helping them. Ends with a "how many beats? is this measure full?" quiz.
