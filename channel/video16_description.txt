What is a quaver? Melody and Barnaby show how a crotchet (quarter note) lasts one beat, and how two quavers (eighth notes) fit into one beat: ti-ti! Quavers together are joined by a beam, and a quaver on its own has a flag. Clap along with every rhythm, then try a quick quiz. A simple music lesson for kids, with the American names too.

For children aged about 4 to 7 learning to read music, and the grown-ups helping them.
