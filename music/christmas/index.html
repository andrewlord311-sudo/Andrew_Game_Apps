The Nativity Advent Calendar

A window a day tells the Christmas story, in order, from 1 December to Christmas Day. Behind each window is something to do, and a sticker that flies into the big Bethlehem picture.

  • By Christmas Day the whole nativity scene is complete
  • A cheeky elf hides in every window
  • Works on a phone or iPad, nothing to install

Jingle Bells with five fingers

Thumb on Middle C, one finger for each note, then play along with Barnaby, slowly first and then up to speed.

  • Finger numbers 1 to 5
  • The notes C, D, E, F and G
  • Short and long notes: "jingle bells" is short, short, long

What they are really learning

These activities follow the order of our Nuggets lessons: finding notes on the keyboard, finger numbers, steps and skips, and long and short notes. Every carol here is traditional and out of copyright; the arrangements are our own, simplified for small hands.
