Name That Carol

🎄 Listen to the tune. Which carol is it?

Watch the keys light up as each note plays. Then tap the carol you think it is.

Which carol is this?

Brilliant!

For grown-ups: each tune is the opening of a traditional carol, played on the piano from Middle C. Naming a tune from its first notes trains musical memory and listening; watching the keys shows whether the tune steps, skips, goes up or goes down.
